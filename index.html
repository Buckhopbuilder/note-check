What to look for · condition & sales

Inspect the note yourself. The scanner does not grade its condition.

Good signs

  • Sharp, square corners and intact edges.
  • Minimal folds or permanent creases.
  • Clean surfaces, clear printing and an intact clear window.

Wear or damage to check

  • Rounded or bent corners and worn edges.
  • Deep creases or multiple folds.
  • Stains, handwriting, fading or rubbed printing.
  • Tears, pinholes, splits, missing pieces or repairs.

Keep it as found—don’t clean, iron or try to repair it. Condition can affect collector interest, but wear alone does not decide whether a flagged note is worth keeping.
