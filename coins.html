This is a limited watchlist, not a complete Australian coin catalogue. 1c and 2c checks cover only the dates listed above; age alone is not a flag. 5c, 10c, $2 and unlisted designs are unsupported. Mintmarks, other varieties, general minting errors, condition and authenticity are not assessed. A flag does not establish rarity, collector demand or value.
